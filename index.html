Choosing the Right Window Cleaning Company In Calgary

Three things to check before you book anyone to work on your home.

  • Experience and Reputation

    Look for a company with a proven track record. Check online reviews and ask for recommendations. This will help you choose a service with a reputation for high-quality work.

  • Transparent Pricing and Clear Communication

    Ensure the company provides transparent pricing with no hidden fees and communicates clearly throughout the process. A reliable service will offer detailed estimates and keep you informed about the scope of work.

IIIHow often should you get your windows cleaned?
In Calgary, it's recommended to clean your windows at least twice a year. If you live in a dusty area or near many trees, you may need to clean more often to prevent dust buildup and maintain clear views.
IVWhat is the difference between window washing and window cleaning?
Window washing means cleaning just the glass, while window cleaning is more thorough. A full window cleaning service includes cleaning the frames, tracks, and screens.
VDo we clean the sills and frames too?
Yes, we clean window sills and frames to ensure no dust or debris around your windows.
VIWhat is the best way to clean windows?
